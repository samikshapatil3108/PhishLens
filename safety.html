🛡️ Cyber Safety Tips

Stay alert. Stay secure. Stay protected.

🔗 1. Check Links Before Clicking

Carefully check the website address before opening a link. Watch for misspellings and unfamiliar domains.

🔐 2. Never Share OTPs or Passwords

Never share your OTP, password, PIN or CVV with anyone through calls, messages or suspicious websites.

🎁 3. Be Careful With Unexpected Rewards

Messages promising prizes, gifts or lottery winnings may be scams. Verify such offers through official sources.

⚠️ 4. Don't Panic Over Urgent Messages

Scammers may threaten to block or suspend your account. Open the official app or website to verify the claim.

🌐 5. Use Official Websites

Type the official website address yourself or use its verified app instead of trusting unknown links.

📩 6. Verify the Sender

Check the sender's email address or phone number. A familiar display name does not guarantee the message is genuine.

📲 7. Keep Devices Updated

Install security updates and download apps only from trusted sources. Use a screen lock on your device.

🚨 8. Report Suspicious Messages

Don't forward suspicious messages. Report them through the relevant platform or official cybercrime reporting channel.

💡 Remember: If a message seems suspicious, pause and verify it through an official source before taking action. PhishLens provides a basic warning, not a guarantee that a message or URL is safe.
